Prosody turns a poem into music. No account or sign-in is needed, and the app works fully offline.

To try it:
1. Close the welcome card ("Start listening"). An example poem is already loaded.
2. Press Play (the purple button). The music starts within a few seconds and the words light up as they are heard.
3. Use the arrows beside the poem's name to switch between the 18 example poems, or edit the text to write your own: the music follows the words.
4. The tabs at the bottom open the mixer, the sound styles, the reading voice, the A/B mappings and "Under the hood", which shows what each word became.

Background audio (UIBackgroundModes: audio): the music is synthesized on the device by the app's own audio engine, and keeps playing with the screen locked or the app in the background, as a music player does. It starts only when the user presses Play and stops with Stop, a call or an alarm.

Reading voice: in Reading, "recorded readings" plays recordings bundled with the app for the example poems; "system voice" reads with the iPhone's own text-to-speech voices (AVSpeechSynthesizer), on the device.

Privacy: nothing is collected or sent; poems stay on the device. The privacy policy is in the app (about, then "privacy") and at https://prosody.rocks/privacy.
