Privacy

Prosody, on the web at prosody.rocks and as an app for Android and iOS. In effect from 20 November 2026.

In short: Prosody collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The poems you write stay on your device. The developer receives none of your data.

What stays on your device

The poem you are writing, the settings you choose (the sound, the mappings, the reading voice, the theme) and whether you have seen the welcome are kept in the app's own storage on your device, so they are there the next time you open it. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

The music is made on your device, from the poem, by the app itself. The instruments, drums, pronunciation dictionary and recorded readings of the example poems are part of the app.

The reading voice

  • Recorded readings are files inside the app; nothing is sent to read them.
  • The system voice (in the Android and iOS apps) reads with the text-to-speech engine of your phone. The app hands it the line to be read and nothing else. On iOS the voices run on the device. On Android, what happens to the text is up to the speech engine you have chosen in your phone's settings and its own privacy policy; Google's speech engine, for example, reads with on-device voices unless you have chosen a network voice.
  • Neural voices (Kokoro, Piper, Kitten; on the website and in the Android app, not in the iOS app) run on your device too, but their code and voice models must be downloaded first, which the app asks you about, with the size, before it starts. They are downloaded from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and Hugging Face (huggingface.co). As with any download, those services see your device's IP address and what was downloaded; no poem and nothing about you is sent to them. If you never choose a neural voice, nothing is downloaded.

Sharing

If you share a poem, the link carries the poem and its settings inside the address itself. Nothing is uploaded or stored by Prosody: whoever you send it to has the poem in the link. When a shared link is opened at prosody.rocks, the website reads the poem from the link to show its first lines in the link's preview, and keeps nothing. Where you send the link is up to you.

The website

prosody.rocks is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the requests your browser makes, including its IP address, to deliver the pages, under its privacy policy. Prosody adds no cookies, analytics or tracking of its own. The apps do not contact the website; the links they make for sharing point to it.

Children

Prosody collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here, with its date. Questions about privacy, or anything else: me@prosody.rocks.
