Support

Prosody turns a poem into music: on the web at prosody.rocks, and as an app for Android and iOS.

Get in touch

Questions, problems, poems that sound wrong, ideas: write to me@prosody.rocks. Bugs can also be reported on GitHub, where the source is.

Common questions

There is no sound. Press Play and give it a moment: the first time, the instruments are prepared. On an iPhone the silent switch does not mute Prosody; check the volume. A call or an alarm stops the music; press Play again afterwards.

The voice sounds robotic. In Reading, choose another voice. On an iPhone, better voices (Enhanced and Premium) can be downloaded in Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices, and Prosody offers them as soon as they are there.

Does it need the internet? No. Everything, the example poems' recorded readings included, is in the app. On Android and the website, the optional neural voices are downloaded the first time you choose one, after asking.

What happens to my poems? They stay on your device. See privacy.

How does it work? Open "about" in the app, or read the design on GitHub.
